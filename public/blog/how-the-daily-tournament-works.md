# How the start.ng daily tournament works

Every day, start.ng picks a few games and runs a tournament on them for 24 hours. The best player wins the day. It's free, and you can enter from your phone.

## Five games, 24 hours, one winner

Each tournament has **5 games** from the community, chosen at random from games that meet start.ng's game standards. It runs for **24 hours** and ends at **18:00 Nigeria time**, when the next one starts.

You'll find today's games, the time left and the standings on [the tournament page](/tournament).

## How to enter

1. Sign in on start.ng.
2. **Share the tournament on X** from the tournament page. That enters you.
3. Play the day's games. Your runs on those games count from then until the tournament ends.

## How points work

Each game in the tournament has its own ranking, from the best run each player makes on it that day. Your place on each game gives you points:

| Place on a game | Points |
|---|---|
| 1st | 10 |
| 2nd | 7 |
| 3rd | 5 |
| 4th | 3 |
| Everyone else who plays it | 1 |

Your points from all five games are added up. **The most points wins the day.** So it pays to play every game, not just your favourite: even a single run on a game is worth a point.

## The overall leaderboard

Every day's points also go on the [overall leaderboard](/leaderboard), all time and for this week. A good finish today keeps counting after the day is over.

## Tournaments with your friends

Want your own tournament? Start one on [start.ng/friends](/friends): pick any games, set how long it runs, and share the link. Your friends join and you all follow one table of scores.

## Make a game for the tournament

Tournament games come from the community. Publish a game that meets the game standards, and it can be picked. See [how to publish a browser game](/blog/publish-a-browser-game) and [the start.ng Kit](/blog/the-start-ng-kit).
